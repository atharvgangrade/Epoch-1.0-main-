The Guild Code

Directives // 2024.HK.THON

01

Sector Confinement

Participants are required to remain within the college premises during the Hackathon. Seeking permission from volunteers is mandatory if there is a critical need to leave the designated sector.

02

Supreme Authority

The decisions made by the judging panel are final and binding. Participants are expected to respect and abide by the outcomes without resistance. This is the way.

03

Conduct Protocol

Any form of misbehavior, whether directed towards team members, volunteers, judges, or evaluators, will not be tolerated. Maintain a collaborative environment.

04

Equipment Loadout

Participants are responsible for bringing their own laptops, chargers, and any other necessary devices required for mission implementation. No armory will be provided.

05

Multi-Domain Integration

Projects must span at least two specified domains. It is essential to integrate diverse aspects into the project to showcase a comprehensive skill set.

06

Practical Utility

Projects should be designed with real-life applications in mind, addressing practical issues and providing tangible solutions for the galaxy.

07

Open Research Access

The Hackathon prioritizes research over rote memorization. Internet use is encouraged for inclusive collaboration and fostering innovation.
